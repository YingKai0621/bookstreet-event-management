## 5. Delivered business behavior

- Participant joins/leaves an Event and sees durable My Events history without an Event QR.
- Activity registration auto-joins atomically, protects capacity, reports schedule overlap and
  supports eligible cancel/re-register on one durable identity.
- One Activity-owned QR is displayed at the venue. The authenticated Participant scans it within
  `[startAt - attendanceEarlyScanMinutes, endAt)`; raw tokens are concealed from public and
  Participant list/detail responses.
- Completion classifies eligible remaining registrations as No-show and issues Certificates
  idempotently. Correction history is append-only and preserves cancellation precedence.
- Participants list/download durable Certificates, create/update one eligible Rating and receive
  owner-only deduplicated Notification rows with mark-one and mark-all read behavior.
- Organizer monitors owned Event/Activity participation and performs confirmed, reasoned,
  idempotent Attendance correction through canonical APIs.
