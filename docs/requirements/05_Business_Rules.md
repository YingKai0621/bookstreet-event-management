**BUSINESS RULES**

Short, plain-language rules for the active MVP baseline

**QR window amendment (2026-10-26):** Organizer may set `attendanceEarlyScanMinutes` per Activity from 0 to 1440. If omitted, it is 15. BR-45 and BR-94 use that stored value; Organizer cannot change it after the first `VALID` Attendance. The closing boundary remains exclusive at `endAt`.

**Role-scoped Notification amendment (2026-11-03):** BR-102 through BR-109 activate the Sprint 10
audience, producer, transaction, read-state, suppression and channel boundaries without changing existing
domain lifecycle meaning.

**Actionable Notification amendment (2026-11-06):** BR-110 adds four approved Sprint 10 producers through
V17. BR-103, BR-104 and BR-105 are read together with BR-110.

**Live inbox update amendment (2026-11-06):** BR-111 adds one content-free Server-Sent Events signal, and
BR-109 no longer excludes SSE.

**System Admin amendment (2026-11-07, active for Sprint 11):** BR-112 through BR-120 define account,
role, PartnerOrganization status, audit, dashboard and password-reset behavior. BR-76 still governs
newly granted roles.

**Booth Payment history amendment (2026-11-10, active for Sprint 11):** BR-121 adds a read-only Booth
Payment history for Admin across all Events and for each Organizer over the Events they own. It adds no
table, status or Payment command; BR-58 through BR-61 keep governing Payment behavior.

# 1. Cách đọc

Business Rule là quy tắc mà hệ thống và người dùng phải tuân theo. Bản này ưu tiên câu ngắn, một ý mỗi rule. “Static” là rule cố định; “Dynamic” là rule phụ thuộc cấu hình, thời gian, trạng thái hoặc từng Event/Activity. Rule có nhãn `POST-MVP REFERENCE` chỉ giữ định hướng tương lai, không được dùng làm Acceptance Criterion, API hoặc migration cho MVP hiện tại.
