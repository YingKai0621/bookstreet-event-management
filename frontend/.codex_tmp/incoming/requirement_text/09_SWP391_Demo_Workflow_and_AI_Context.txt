3. Hero Demo Flow — luồng xương sống của hệ thống
Đây là luồng nên được coi là “câu chuyện chính” khi demo hội đồng. Nó cho thấy integration giữa Organizer, Partner, Guest/Participant, Booth, Activity, Booth QR Visit, Activity QR Attendance và Rewards.
1. Organizer tạo Event, cấu hình thời gian/deadline/Location/Booth và Publish.
↓
2. Partner đăng nhập và Apply to Event.
↓
3. Organizer Review Partner Application và Approve.
↓
4. Partner mở Booth Map, chọn Booth A05 đang AVAILABLE và gửi Booth Request.
↓
5. Hệ thống tạo Active Booth Hold; Organizer Review và Approve → tạo Booth Assignment.
↓
6. Partner Submit Activity Proposal (title, exact time, Location, capacity, note).
↓
7. Organizer Review, hệ thống check hard conflict, Organizer Approve rồi Publish Activity.
↓
8. Guest nhìn thấy Event/Activity trên public website; khi muốn đăng ký thì Sign In.
↓
9. Participant Register Activity; hệ thống kiểm tra deadline + capacity và tạo ActivityRegistration. Nếu chưa có EventRegistration thì hệ thống auto-create.
↓
10. Participant ghé Booth đang được assign, tự quét QR của BoothAssignment → hệ thống tạo BoothVisit và cộng Point tối đa một lần cho lượt ghé hợp lệ.
↓
11. Tại Activity, Participant đã đăng ký tự quét Activity QR trong time window hợp lệ → Attendance = ATTENDED.
↓
12. Hệ thống kiểm tra total Points để unlock Badge; nếu Activity hỗ trợ Certificate và Attendance hợp lệ thì cấp Certificate. Attendance cũng là căn cứ cho ActivityRating.
↓
13. Organizer mở Dashboard/Report và thấy BoothVisit, Point/Badge, registrations, Attendance/no-show và các số liệu Event được cập nhật.

4. Exception Paths — bắt buộc chuẩn bị
Milestone 2 yêu cầu core transaction có ít nhất 2 exception paths. Team nên code tối thiểu 3–4 case để vừa đáp ứng rubric vừa có phương án dự phòng khi demo.

Expected behavior
Giá trị khi demo

Tạo Hold atomically; chỉ một Partner thành công. Người còn lại nhận thông báo Booth vừa được giữ.
Chứng minh concurrency + transaction DB, không chỉ validation UI.

5. QR Participation — flow đã chốt
Có hai QR flow tách biệt. Activity QR thuộc Activity: Participant có ActivityRegistration hợp lệ tự quét trong time window để tạo Attendance = ATTENDED. Booth QR thuộc BoothAssignment đang active: Participant tự quét khi ghé Booth để tạo BoothVisit; QR hết hiệu lực khi Assignment/Event kết thúc.
Trường hợp
Người quét
Luồng
Activity QR
Participant
Tự scan Activity QR → validate ActivityRegistration + time window → Attendance = ATTENDED.
BoothAssignment QR
Participant
Tự scan Booth QR → validate active Assignment/Event → BoothVisit → Point tối đa một lần cho lượt ghé hợp lệ.
Sửa Attendance
Organizer có quyền correction/override
Chỉ dùng khi cần; phải lưu người xử lý, thời điểm và lý do correction/override.
5.1 Validation cần chạy khi Participant tự scan
Activity QR có map đúng Activity không, và Participant có ActivityRegistration active cho Activity đó không?
Activity QR có nằm trong time window hợp lệ và Activity còn hiệu lực không?
ActivityRegistration/Activity có bị CANCELLED không?
Booth QR có map tới BoothAssignment đang ACTIVE trong Event hiện tại không?
Participant đã có BoothVisit được tính thưởng cho EventRegistration + BoothAssignment này chưa?
Scan lặp có tạo duplicate Attendance, BoothVisit, Point hoặc Certificate không? Kết quả phải idempotent theo rule.

9. AI Context Baseline — copy cho AI khi làm task mới
Mục đích
Phần này là context cô đọng. Khi dùng ChatGPT/Codex/AI khác cho requirement, ERD, code, test hoặc UI, hãy đưa phần này trước để AI không tự mở rộng scope hoặc hiểu sai domain.
Key
Baseline context
System
Book Street Event Management System for ONE fixed physical Book Street. Multiple Events happen over time and share physical Locations/Booths.
Core actors
Guest, Participant, Partner/Exhibitor, Event Organizer, System Admin. Do not add Staff as a core actor in MVP.
Event ownership
Events belong to the Book Street management system, not to the Organizer account that created them.
Partner gate
Partner must be approved to join an Event before Request Booth or Submit Activity Proposal.
Booth model
Physical Booth is not the same as Booth Request, Booth Hold, or Booth Assignment. Availability is evaluated by Event/time; do not store a single global ASSIGNED status that blocks future non-overlapping Events.
Booth flow
Partner selects a specific AVAILABLE Booth on the map → Request + Active Hold → Organizer approves/rejects → Assignment or release.
Booth expiration
If active Hold expires while request is pending: Request → EXPIRED, Hold released, Booth available again, expired request cannot be approved.
Booth concurrency
Create Hold / confirm Assignment atomically; no two valid holds/assignments may conflict on the same Booth for overlapping time.
Activity model
Activity is a program; Location is where it happens. Activity may be at a Partner Booth, Main Stage, Workshop Area, etc. Partner does not need a Booth to propose an Activity.
Activity proposal
Partner proposes; Organizer reviews; approval and publication are separate concepts. Organizer/BTC may create its own Activity directly.
Activity conflict
Hard block same physical Location + overlapping time for locked/approved activities, including across different Events sharing the Book Street.
Participant
Participant can Join Event for My Events/notifications and separately Register Activity for a capacity-controlled slot. If needed, EventRegistration may auto-create when registering an Activity or scanning a valid BoothAssignment QR.
QR attendance
Two QR flows: Activity QR belongs to Activity; a registered Participant self-scans in the valid time window to create Attendance = ATTENDED. Booth QR belongs to an active BoothAssignment; Participant self-scans when visiting to create BoothVisit. No Attendance Operator scanning in MVP.
Attendance
Each ActivityRegistration has at most one Attendance. MVP does not require check-in + check-out or attendance duration. Duplicate Activity QR scans must not create duplicate Attendance, Certificate or ActivityRating.
Rewards
Points come from valid BoothVisit and accumulate across Events. Badge is unlocked by total Points threshold. Certificate requires valid Activity Attendance + certificateAvailable. ActivityRating also requires valid Attendance.
Cancellation
Pending request/proposal may be withdrawn directly. Approved/assigned/published items use Cancellation Request; Organizer confirms/processes. Late cancellation is flagged after cutoff; do not auto-cancel without Organizer processing.
MVP scope lock
No payment gateway, paid tickets/booths, waitlist, staff scheduling, asset inventory, sponsor module, reward store, AI recommendation/chatbot, advanced BI, legal digital-signature certificate, or advanced Map Editor/multi-map authoring.
Demo priority
Build end-to-end workflow first: Event → Partner → Booth/Activity → Participant Registration → Booth QR Visit + Activity QR Attendance → Points/Badge/Certificate → Dashboard. Public/Heritage/Feedback/Admin extras are secondary.
SWP391 expectation
Workflow 0 security, Workflow 1 CRUD/data setup, Workflow 2 core success + ≥2 exceptions, Workflow 3 dashboard/reporting; MVC/multi-layer, relational DB, testing and AI evidence.
9.1 Instruction cho AI
Use this document as the current team baseline. Do not invent features, actors, states or payment/legal flows outside it.
When a request conflicts with the baseline, flag the conflict before changing requirements.
Keep terminology consistent: Event, Activity, Location, Booth, Booth Request, Booth Hold, Booth Assignment, Activity Proposal, Activity Registration, Attendance.
For implementation advice, always connect UI → Controller → Service/business rule → Repository/DB → state update → notification/report impact where relevant.
For test design, include both success path and exception/concurrency/deadline cases.
Prefer simple MVP-safe implementation over adding a new module unless the requirement explicitly needs it.
